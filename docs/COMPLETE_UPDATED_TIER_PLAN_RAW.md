## 1. Purpose and fixed-scope principle

This plan covers three fixed website tiers for **service-based businesses**. Every tier is designed to present a credible brand, explain services clearly, direct visitors to an appropriate next step, and provide a complete responsive experience across mobile, tablet, and desktop.

The fixed scope protects both the client and provider: each tier states its public-page limit, approved content capacity, conversion workflow, included setup work, and exclusions before production begins. The client provides accurate business information and approvals; the provider performs the agreed configuration, design, implementation, quality assurance, and launch preparation.

> **Service-only boundary:** These tiers do not include product catalogues, shopping carts, checkout, online payments, inventory, fulfillment, delivery management, or e-commerce order operations.

## 4. Tier 1 — Essential Service Website

### Investment and purpose

**₱9,900** for exactly **3 public pages**. Tier 1 is for a local provider, clinic, studio, professional, or service business that needs a polished public presence and a dependable way to capture service, appointment, meeting, quote, or general inquiry requests.

### Fixed public-page structure

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **1. Home** | Brand introduction, hero message, value proposition, service highlights, selected visual content, proof points based only on approved facts, Google Reviews call to action, and primary conversion action. |
| **2. Services** | Up to 6 approved services with concise descriptions, selected imagery where supplied, and clear request actions. |
| **3. Contact / Request a Service** | Service request, appointment request, meeting request, quote request, or general inquiry form; verified contact details; fallback contact route; and clear next-step messaging. |

### Included scope

Tier 1 includes responsive UI/UX, up to 6 approved services, up to 8 approved images, up to 3 genuine client-supplied and permission-cleared proof items, up to 5 approved FAQs where appropriate, complete approved footer, Google Reviews pathway, and two consolidated revision rounds.

The provider creates and configures the Google Sheet request tracker and connects the website form through a server-mediated flow. The form can collect client-approved fields such as name, contact information, selected service, preferred date/time suggestion, message, and consent. Validation, loading feedback, truthful success/error messaging, and a fallback contact path are included.

### Tier 1 request workflow

> Visitor sends a service or appointment request → the website records the request in the configured Google Sheet → the business reviews it and follows up directly.

The Google Sheet records a request. It does **not** reserve a live slot, confirm an appointment, process payment, issue invoices, manage service delivery, create a calendar event, or operate as a full CRM.

### Tier 1 client inputs

The client provides business name, niche, target audience, approved domain options, business description, key message, primary call to action, up to 6 services, verified contact details, hours, service area or directions link, social links, approved logo, up to 8 approved images, form purpose and fields, fallback contact path, Google email address for tracker access, Google review destination, and final written approval.

## 9. General exclusions and integrity rules

The following are excluded from every tier unless separately agreed in writing:

- Fabricated, purchased, copied, or unverified testimonials, ratings, reviews, case studies, or customer claims.
- Review scraping, automated copying of Google reviews, or a promise of a particular review rating, number of reviews, Google ranking, traffic, lead volume, or sales outcome.
- E-commerce catalogues, carts, checkout, payments, inventory, fulfillment, delivery operations, and order management.
- Ongoing advertising, social-media management, content marketing, SEO campaigns, backlink services, legal review, regulatory certification, or ongoing support plans not expressly stated in a separate agreement.
- Premium domain purchase costs, renewal fees after the first included year, third-party subscription costs, booking-platform fees, payment-provider fees, external platform charges, and paid media costs.
- Major copywriting, photography, videography, branding, translation, new integrations, custom automations, extra pages, extra revision rounds, or expanded dashboards beyond the approved tier scope.
