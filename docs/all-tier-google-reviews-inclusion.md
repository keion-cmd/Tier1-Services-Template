# Complimentary Google Reviews Pathway — All Tiers

Every website tier includes one **Google Reviews pathway** at no additional charge. This inclusion uses the client-approved Google Business Profile or Google Maps review destination, so visitors can submit authentic reviews through Google rather than a fabricated on-site testimonial system.

| Tier | Complimentary inclusion | Delivery boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | A polished **Review us on Google** call to action in the homepage Reviews area, opening the supplied Google review destination in a new tab. | One client-approved review destination and link verification. No on-site review storage, review moderation, or review scraping. |
| **Tier 2** | A branded Google Review call to action placed in the agreed conversion area or footer, opening the supplied Google review destination in a new tab. | One client-approved review destination and link verification. No on-site review storage, review moderation, or review scraping. |
| **Tier 3** | A branded Google Review call to action incorporated into the public website’s agreed conversion journey, opening the supplied Google review destination in a new tab. | One client-approved review destination and link verification. No on-site review storage, review moderation, or review scraping. |

## Client input and exclusions

The client supplies the verified direct Google review link or Google Business Profile/Maps link and confirms that they are authorized to use it. Google controls sign-in, review publication, ratings, review policy enforcement, and any review-removal process. The complimentary pathway does not create, seed, copy, scrape, edit, or guarantee reviews or ratings.
